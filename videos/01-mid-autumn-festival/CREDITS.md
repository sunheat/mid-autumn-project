## Chapter-bell sound

`assets/audio/chapter-bell.wav` is a processed three-second excerpt of the c1 sound from [A Novel Physical Modeling Approach for Sound Synthesis of Chinese Chime Bells](https://github.com/wizonce9/A-Novel-Physical-Modeling-Approach-for-Sound-Synthesis-of-Chinese-Chime-Bells).

The source repository credits **Nie Kaiwen and Li Yunpeng, Wuhan Conservatory of Music**, for sampling support and **Wu Chih-Wei, Georgia Institute of Technology**, for technical guidance. Its README declares MIT; its linked standalone LICENSE file was unavailable when checked on 13 October 2026. The upstream README declaration is the recorded license evidence. This sound is not relicensed under CC BY 4.0.

Processing retained pitch and speed, selected a three-second decay, adjusted level, and attenuated the tail. The same source is used at both chapter boundaries. It is a research synthesis/sample source; no claim is made that this particular file is an unprocessed live recording.

## Voices and illustrated artwork

The accepted narration uses a synthesized Sulafat voice. The separate question uses Callirrhoe, and the short spoken ending uses the accepted Sulafat take. Existing recordings and level processing are preserved as independently editable audio files.

Illustrations were produced with image-generation tools and reference-guided edits. Typography, educational diagrams, collages, and small character/ornament elements were composed locally and retained in the final scene images. The generated family images are illustrations, not documentary photographs of actual people.

The release thumbnail in `publishing/thumbnail.jpg` is an AI-assisted adaptation of the film's Chang'e illustration with the words "WHY THE MOON?". It is illustrated artwork, not a documentary photograph. No font files are distributed; the font name in the styled subtitle files is a reference to a locally installed font.
